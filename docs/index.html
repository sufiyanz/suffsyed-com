Qubit Teams: The Future Built by Two

Passage 4 / Opening · 57 words, unabridged.

In the era of high-performance product cultures, when every release demanded painstaking coordination, “two-pizza teams” became a way to import startup speed into lumbering corporations. A self-contained group of eight to ten could move without drowning in process. Scarcity sharpened judgment. Fewer voices meant clearer decisions. The pizza was a metaphor for constraint as much as camaraderie.
